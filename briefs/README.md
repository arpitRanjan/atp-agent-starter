# Briefs

Before each piece of work, write a short brief for your AI coding tool and save it here as `stage-NN-what.md`. A good brief says:

1. **What to build**, in one or two sentences.
2. **The rules it must follow** (limits, what needs approval, which files to change and which to leave alone).
3. **How you will check it** (the test, the eval task, what you will look for in the trace).

The brief is your PM artifact: the code is the AI tool's job, the brief and the check are yours.
